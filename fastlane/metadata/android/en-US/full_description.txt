Fluxer is an open source chat app for friends, groups and communities. Text, voice, video and screen sharing in one place, with no ads and nothing tracking you.

Talk one to one, in a group chat, or in a community organised into channels. Give people roles with the permissions they need, and moderate it yourself.

Choose who can message you, call you or see your profile. Block and report anyone.

Host Fluxer yourself for your family, school, business or friends, or use fluxer.app. The app signs in to either.

Fluxer and Fluxer mobile are in open public beta, so things are still being polished.

GitHub beta APKs include optional crash reporting when you opt in. F-Droid builds do not include crash reporting.
